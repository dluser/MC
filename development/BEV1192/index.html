Draught System Components: guided widgets

Seven focused widgets, one per topic in the handout, in the same order. Each one runs Learn → Try it → Check, fits its frame with no scrollbars, has a full-screen button, and embeds on its own Brightspace page. Pick a topic to preview it, then copy its embed code.

Why each activity type

Each hands-on activity matches how its content works on the job. Learners act directly on the illustration, not on separate buttons.

1 · Gas

Cause-and-effect simulation

Pressure differential is a cause-and-effect idea, so learners open the regulator shut-off and pull the tap on the drawing and watch what flows.

2 · Safety

Drag-to-place scenario + exploratory sim

Storage is a placement decision: drag the cylinder itself from the delivery van into a room, and tap it to tip it over. CO₂ exposure is a progression: open the leak and watch the worker's symptoms build, then ventilate.

3 · Regulators

Control-panel simulation

Setting pressure is a hands-on skill, so the regulators have rotary knobs and live gauges. An elevation map makes the altitude rule concrete.

4 · Tubing

Image drag-and-drop (two rounds)

Choosing tubing means matching parts to places. A labeled side view makes direct draw and long draw unmistakable. Drag tubes onto the runs, then lines onto cylinders, one round per screen.

5 · Couplers

Predict → observe → explain

The check valve is easy to misunderstand, so learners predict first, then pull the lever, cut the gas and remove the valve, and watch the result.

6 · Kegs

Comparison explorer + investigation

Sizes are a comparison, with details popping up on the keg you tap. Diagnosing a foamy keg is detective work: a checklist fills in as you gather clues, then you make the call.

7 · Faucets

Exploded view + categorize

The handout's own parts diagram becomes an exploded faucet whose numbers show each part's name on hover or tap. The style trade-offs become a true sort into two bins.

Throughout

Built for adults, built for everyone

Job-based hooks, one idea per screen, practice before the check, explained feedback with a retry, and a recap. Fully keyboard and screen-reader operable, with no violations in automated WCAG 2.1 AA scans at desktop and phone sizes.

New in v5

  • Glasses sit under the faucet spouts in every picture.
  • Try it feedback moved to the top of the picture, right under the instructions, and glows when it changes.
  • Checks: after a wrong answer, pick another answer right away or press Try again.
  • Regulators and Couplers callouts: click a number to keep its label on; click again to hide it.
  • The Inter font is bundled with the widgets, so nothing shifts while it loads and no outside font service is needed.
  • Dragging a zoomed picture no longer highlights it.

New in v4

  • Taller design height (900px) with the picture on top and text below.
  • Learner zoom on lesson and question pictures: buttons, drag to pan, or Ctrl/⌘ + scroll.
  • Every picture checked for labels covering labels or parts; overlaps fixed throughout.
  • Kegs redrawn as smooth shapes; cylinders are solid unless their contents matter.
  • Faucet spouts point down; the dragged cylinder in Gas Safety floats above the rooms.
  • Tubing practice is now two drags: direct draw first, then long draw, each with one tube that doesn't belong.
  • Numbered hover markers pulse until explored; overview checkmarks replay every 7 seconds; logos stay put.
