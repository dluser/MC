People can participate in Creative Commons by openly licensing their own work, joining community discussions on Zulip, participating in the CC Global Network, contributing to Open Education and Open Culture initiatives, attending events, and becoming CC Certified. These activities help strengthen the global commons and support a culture of sharing.
