Photo / Video Consent and Release

Innovation and Instructional Support (IIS) • Central New Mexico Community College

Consent to be Photographed / Filmed

Complete this form when CNM (IIS) captures photos, audio, or video for instructional content, training, and CNM-related communications.

1) Purpose of Recording

CNM’s Department of Innovation and Instructional Support (IIS) creates instructional and educational media (e.g., course videos, training modules, demonstrations, and learning resources) to support students, faculty, and staff. This consent authorizes CNM to record and use your image/likeness and/or voice in those materials.

2) Participant Information

3) Consent and Release

I authorize Central New Mexico Community College (CNM) and its Department of Innovation and Instructional Support (IIS) (including CNM employees, contractors, and authorized representatives) to photograph, record, and/or film me, and to use my image, likeness, voice, and/or statements (“Media”) as described below.

  • Permitted uses: Educational, instructional, training, informational, and CNM-related communications (including recruitment and college promotions when applicable).
  • Distribution channels: CNM websites, CNM learning platforms (including the LMS), internal communications, presentations, print materials, and CNM-controlled social media.
  • Editing and formats: CNM may edit, reproduce, display, publish, and distribute the Media, in whole or in part, in any medium now known or later developed, consistent with CNM policy and applicable law.
  • Ownership: I understand CNM retains ownership of the recordings and resulting materials.
  • No compensation: I acknowledge I will not receive payment or other compensation for the use of the Media.
  • Release of liability: I release and hold harmless CNM, its governing board, officers, employees, and agents from claims arising from the authorized use of the Media, including claims for invasion of privacy, right of publicity, or misappropriation, except where prohibited by law.
  • FERPA notice (students): If I am a student, I understand CNM will handle student information consistent with applicable privacy laws and CNM policy. CNM will not intentionally publish protected student information beyond what is authorized or necessary for the educational purpose.
  • Public records: I understand certain CNM records may be subject to inspection/disclosure under applicable public records laws, to the extent required by law.
  • Voluntary consent and revocation: I understand this consent is voluntary. If I wish to revoke consent for future use, I may submit a written request to IIS; however, revocation may not apply to Media already produced, published, distributed, or incorporated into course materials prior to CNM’s receipt of my request.
  • Accessibility: I understand CNM may add captions/transcripts or other accessibility features to recordings.

4) Optional Restrictions

If you want to limit usage (example: “internal training only” or “no social media”), list restrictions here. If blank, CNM may use the Media as described above.

5) Term of Consent

This consent is effective indefinitely unless revoked in writing as described in Section 3 (Voluntary consent and revocation).
