About the IDEA Studio

The IDEA Studio (Innovation, Discovery, Educational Advancement) empowers faculty and subject matter experts to explore new ways of teaching through technology, digital media, and thoughtful learning design. Supported by Learning Experience Designers, the studio provides guidance, collaboration, and creative tools to help translate ideas into engaging, accessible learning experiences.

Working with Learning Experience Designers (LXD's)

Tip: You can use the arrow keys on your keyboard (← / →) to move between steps.

1. Explore

We start by understanding your teaching goals, learners, and course context. As well as any challenges or root issues you face.

2. Design

Together, we plan an engaging learning experience and choose the right mix of media, tools, and interactivity.

3. Create

We help build and refine course materials, from videos and interactive activities to immersive learning experiences.

4. Review

You review and test the materials, and we make refinements to ensure clarity, accessibility, and usability.

5. Launch

Final materials are delivered and integrated into your course so you’re ready to teach with confidence.

6. Improve

After students engage with the experience, we reflect, gather feedback, and support future improvements.
